Browser validators. `validate="required | minLength:3"` on a named field gives instant feedback before a form is sent. The available validators are `required`, `email`, `url`, `minLength`, `maxLength`, `min`, `max`, `pattern` and `in`, and the check refuses unknown or malformed ones. Messages go into the field's `@error` spot, which also gets `setCustomValidity`. Submitting with an invalid field is stopped and focuses the first one. A page with a `validate` field ships one small validator script (`/_kick/c/kick-validate.<hash>.js`); other pages ship none. **`@error` output changes.** `@error('field')` now always renders a `<span data-kick-error="field" aria-live="polite">` spot, hidden while there's no error, so its output goes from the bare body to the body inside that span. Keep `@error` bodies inline (text, `<b>`, `<small>`): a `<p>`, `<li>` or `<tr>` body was valid before and isn't inside a `<span>`, and a table moves it out. Move block markup outside the `@error`, or change it to inline elements.
