`kick new --template inertia`: an app whose pages are React components rendered through `@forinda/kickjs-inertia`, with server-side rendering, an error page, Tailwind-ready styles and aliases from `tsconfig.json` `paths`, alongside the usual `/api/v1` modules, on any runtime. Scaffold layers can now add package.json scripts through `feature.json`.
